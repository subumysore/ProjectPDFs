Passkey (WebAuthn) — hardware-backed unlock

Enrol a passkey so your vault unlocks with your device's authenticator (Windows Hello, Touch ID, or a security key). The key is derived from the passkey's PRF secret, which only exists when the hardware is present — so even a malicious extension update can't decrypt your vault without it. Enrol on an empty vault (or before adding data).

Migrating re-encrypts your existing vault under the passkey. Unlock it first (open the popup, enter your passphrase), then click Migrate.
