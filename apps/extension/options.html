Passkey (WebAuthn) — hardware-backed unlock

Enrol a passkey so your vault unlocks with your device's authenticator (Windows Hello, Touch ID, or a security key). The key is derived from the passkey's PRF secret, which only exists when the hardware is present — so even a malicious extension update can't decrypt your vault without it. Enrol on an empty vault (or before adding data).

Migrating re-encrypts your existing vault under the passkey. Unlock it first (open the popup, enter your passphrase), then click Migrate.

Your base language

The language you think in. Forms in another language will be translated to this for viewing, and your answers translated back into the form's language when filling — all on-device. Install the matching language pack below to enable translation.

Single vault (desktop app)

If you use the desktop app too, make it your single source of truth: the extension reads and writes your details through the desktop app's vault over a local bridge — keys never live in the extension, and you never retype your data in two places. Requires the desktop app installed and its companion registered.

Translation languages

Nothing is bundled. A language's model (~110 MB each direction) downloads only the first time you translate that pair — then it's cached on your device. Download here to pre-load one for offline use. Everything runs on-device; only the model file is fetched, never your data.
