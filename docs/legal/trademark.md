## Goods/Services classification (Nice)
- **Class 9** — downloadable computer software for form-filling, PDF/document processing,
  optical character recognition, on-device translation, and data management.
- **Class 42** — software-as-a-service / providing non-downloadable software; design and
  development of computer software.

## Suggested filing strategy
1. **Trademark search first** (India TM registry + target markets) for `PolyglotFormFill` and any
   confusingly similar marks in Classes 9/42.
2. **India** — file with the Trade Marks Registry (word mark + logo, Classes 9 & 42). Startup/
   individual fee is modest.
3. **Priority abroad** — if selling in the US/EU, consider filing there within 6 months to claim
   the India priority date (Paris Convention), or a Madrid Protocol international application.
4. Use the **™** symbol now (unregistered); switch to **®** only after registration grants.

## Brand-use guidelines (protect distinctiveness)
- Always capitalize as **PolyglotFormFill** (one word, internal caps).
- Use as an adjective + generic noun where practical ("the PolyglotFormFill app"), not as a verb.
- Keep the logo's proportions and teal palette consistent.
