Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026
In one sentence: ProjectPDFs processes everything — reading forms, OCR, translation, filling, and signing — on your device, and we never receive your documents or the data you enter. The only data that leaves is a form you choose to submit (sent directly to its recipient) or a web search you type — never through us.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your documents, forms, or the values in your vault.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, or content-bearing crash reports.
  • There's no account to create and no upload. App updates, fonts, and language models download one-way to your device — the way any app updates itself — but there is no channel for your documents or vault to be sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device (only)

  • Your vault (profiles and field values) is stored locally and AES-256 encrypted. The key is derived from your passphrase or a passkey (WebAuthn) and is never stored and never sent anywhere.
  • Filled documents are saved where you choose, on your device.

The only times data leaves your device — because you chose to

1. Submit online. When you click Submit, the completed form goes directly from your device to the recipient you chose (e.g. a government or vendor site). We never proxy, see, or store it.
2. Web search. If you use the search box to find a form, the search terms you type go directly to a privacy-respecting search engine (DuckDuckGo). Only those terms are sent — never your documents, vault, or identifiers. This is optional; you can paste a URL instead.

That is the complete list. There is no other transmission of your data.

Permissions (browser extension)

  • storage — keep your encrypted vault on your device.
  • activeTab + scripting — fill the form on the page you're viewing, only when you click.
  • nativeMessaging — communicate with the optional ProjectPDFs desktop app on your own computer.
  • No always-on page access, no remote code (Manifest V3), no host permissions for background access.

Licensing

Paid features are unlocked by an offline license verified on your device. Verifying a license does not contact us and sends nothing.

Children

ProjectPDFs is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We'll update this page and the "last updated" date for any material change.
