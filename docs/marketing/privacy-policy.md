## 2. Scope and definitions
- **"Personal Data"** means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
- **"User Content"** means the documents, forms, images, and the values you enter or store in the Service
  (your "vault"), including any Personal Data contained therein.
- **"Device"** means the computer or device on which you run the Service.
- **"Process"** has the meaning given under applicable data-protection law (collection, storage, use,
  transmission, etc.).

## 3. Our core privacy principle (the substance of this Policy)
The Service is designed so that **we do not see, store, or transmit your User Content, and we do not send
it to any location you did not intend.** Specifically:

1. **On-device processing.** All operations on User Content — reading and rendering documents, optical
   character recognition (OCR), translation, field detection, form filling and export, and digital
   signing — are performed **locally on your Device**. We do not receive your User Content to perform them.
2. **No collection by us.** We do **not** collect, receive, access, or store your User Content or the
   Personal Data within it. There is **no user account** required to use the core Service and **no upload**
   of your User Content to us.
3. **User-directed transmission only.** User Content leaves your Device **only** when you initiate a
   specific action, and only to the destination **you** choose:
   - **(a) Submitting a completed form** — the form is transmitted **directly from your Device to the
     recipient you select** (for example, a government or vendor website). We do not proxy, intercept,
     receive, retain, or have access to that transmission.
   - **(b) Web search (optional)** — if you use the in-Service search to locate a form, **only the search
     terms you type** are sent, directly from your Device to the third-party search provider
     (**DuckDuckGo**), to return results. No User Content, vault data, or identifiers are sent. You may
     avoid this entirely by entering a URL instead.
   These are the **only** circumstances in which User Content or search terms leave your Device. Each is
   user-initiated, clearly labelled in the interface, and directed to a destination you selected.

## 4. Data we do process (limited, non-content)
Depending on how you obtain and use the Service, the following limited, **non-content** processing may occur:
- **Software distribution & updates.** When you download or update the Service, or when the Service
  downloads assets it needs (application updates, fonts, language/OCR models), your Device makes a
  one-way request to the relevant distribution channel (e.g. the applicable app store or content host).
  Such requests may involve technical data (e.g. IP address) processed by that channel as an independent
  controller under its own policy. This is a **download to your Device**; it provides no pathway for your
  User Content to be uploaded. [Confirm the exact hosts and whether any are operated by us.]
- **Payments & licensing.** Paid features are unlocked by an **offline, cryptographically-signed license**
  verified **on your Device**; license verification contacts no server and transmits nothing. If you
  purchase a license, the payment is processed by our payment provider **not applicable (no paid features yet)** as an independent
  controller/processor; we receive only the information necessary to issue and support your license
  (e.g. name, email, order id) — never your User Content. [Confirm provider and data elements.]
- **Voluntary communications.** If you contact support, we process the information you provide to respond.

We do **not** use analytics, advertising identifiers, tracking technologies, or content-bearing crash
reporting. [Confirm no telemetry SDKs are included in any build.]

software; and consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) where required (e.g. optional features). Because we do not process

## 6. How your on-device data is protected
- Your vault is stored **only on your Device** and is **encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).**
- The encryption key is derived on unlock from your passphrase or a hardware passkey (WebAuthn) and is
  **never stored** and never transmitted.
- The browser extension requests least-privilege permissions, contains **no remote code** (Manifest V3),
  and does not access pages in the background.

any party. For the limited non-content data in §4, we share only with the service providers named there,
acting under contract. Any cross-border transfer of such non-content data is governed by that provider's
safeguards (e.g. GDPR Chapter V mechanisms). [Counsel to complete transfer mechanisms and sub-processor
