# ProjectPDFs — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-18. Plain-language summary first; specifics follow._

## The one-sentence version
**Your content and personal data never leave your device.** ProjectPDFs does all of its
work — reading forms, OCR, translation, filling, and signing — on your own device, and we
never receive, store, or transmit your documents or the data you put into them.

## What we do NOT collect
- We do **not** collect your documents, forms, or the values in your vault.
- We do **not** use analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, or crash payloads that
  contain your content.
- We have **no server that receives your content.** Our servers (if any) only serve assets
  *down* to you — app updates, fonts, and models — and never receive your data.

## Data stored on your device (only)
- Your **vault** (profiles and field values) is stored locally and **AES-256 encrypted**. The
  key is derived from your passphrase or a passkey (WebAuthn) and is **never stored** and never
  sent anywhere.
- Filled documents are saved where you choose, on your device.

## The only times data leaves your device — and only because you chose to
1. **Submit online:** when you click Submit, the completed form goes **directly** from your
   device to the recipient you chose (e.g. a government or vendor site). We never proxy, see,
   or store it.
2. **Web search:** if you use the search box to find a form, the **search terms you type** go
   directly to a privacy-respecting search engine (DuckDuckGo) to return results. Only those
   terms are sent — never your documents, vault, or identifiers. This is optional; you can
   paste a URL instead.

That's the complete list. There is no other transmission of your data.

## Permissions (browser extension)
- **storage** — keep your encrypted vault on your device.
- **activeTab + scripting** — fill the form on the page you're viewing, only when you click.
- **nativeMessaging** — communicate with the optional ProjectPDFs desktop app on your own
  computer (your keys/vault can stay there instead of in the extension).
- No always-on page access, no remote code (Manifest V3), no host permissions for background
  access.

## Licensing
Paid features are unlocked by an **offline license** verified on your device. Verifying a
license does not contact us and sends nothing.

## Children
Not directed to children under 13.

We'll update this page and the "last updated" date for any material change.
