The headline feature

Turn a non-fillable PDF into a form you can actually fill

A flat PDF with no form fields — or even a photo or scan of one — becomes a real, fillable form in seconds. No “print, hand-write, re-scan.” No uploading it to some website to do it.

🧩
Non-fillable → fillable

Any flat PDF, scan, or photo becomes an editable form

Point it at a PDF that has no fillable boxes, or even a picture of a paper form. On-device OCR reads the layout, finds where every field belongs, places real form fields there, and fills them from your vault — all on your machine, nothing uploaded. Then export a clean, filled PDF.

🔒

On-device, always

OCR, translation, filling and signing run locally. Your data never travels to us or anyone.

Who it's for

Built for anyone who fills forms across languages and borders

Three groups get the most out of it — and every one of them keeps their data on their own device.

🛂

Immigration & relocation agencies

Turn scanned visa, residency, and settlement PDFs into fillable forms with on-device OCR, and complete them in a client's language without a single document leaving your machine. One saved profile fills the next form in seconds.

💼

Cross-border freelancers & small businesses

Tax, banking, and onboarding forms often arrive in a language that isn't yours. Fill them from your own details — held in an AES-256 vault on your device — and export a clean PDF, with nothing routed through us or any third party.

🛡️

Privacy-first & crypto communities

Every step runs locally with zero telemetry, and the vault unlocks with a hardware passkey (WebAuthn) whose key is never stored. No account, no cloud, no tracking — your data stays on the device you control.

Built for a multilingual world

Your data in your language. The form in theirs.

Governments and institutions publish forms in one language; people hold their details in another. PolyglotFormFill bridges that gap on-device — it fills forms in any language, and translates across English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Arabic and Russian, with more on the way.
