What it does

Everything a form-filler should — without the cloud

Most tools upload your documents to "process" them. PolyglotFormFill does the processing on your machine.

🔒

On-device, always

OCR, translation, filling and signing run locally. Your data never travels to us or anyone.

🧩

Make any PDF fillable

A flat PDF — or a photo/scan of a form with no fields? OCR detects the fields, places them, and fills them.

🌍

Polyglot fill

Fill an English form from your data in another language. Translation happens on your device.

📄

Word & Excel too

Fills .docx and .xlsx forms — named fields or flat labels — and can export a PDF.

🧠

A vault that learns

Answer a new field once; it's remembered — encrypted, on your device — for next time.

✍️

Trustworthy signing

Non-delegable, device-bound signatures with verifiable provenance. Nobody signs on your behalf.

🔑

Passkey-locked vault

Unlock with a passphrase or a hardware passkey (WebAuthn). The key is never stored — not even by us.

🔗

Desktop + browser

The extension fills web forms; the desktop app holds your keys as a hardened trust anchor.

Built for a multilingual world

Your data in your language. The form in theirs.

Governments and institutions publish forms in one language; people hold their details in another. PolyglotFormFill bridges that gap on-device — starting with English ↔ Hindi, with more to come.

Privacy

The promise, in one line

Your data is processed on your device and is never sent to us. The only things that leave are actions you take — submitting a finished form (straight to its recipient) or a web search you type. Those go directly to their destination, never through us. That's the whole list.
